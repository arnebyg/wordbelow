A word game in layers

Every word you take uncovers new letters underneath.

Make words from the open tiles. Take them away, and the letters below come up — so the best word now is the one that leaves a good word next. wordbelow is a word game about thinking one layer ahead.

For iPhone and Android.

Play it your way

Words under every tile

Letters lie in layers. Every word you take uncovers new ones, and a tile is open only when nothing lies on top of it.

Today's board

The same letters for every player, ten minutes on the clock, one leaderboard and a streak to keep.

Friends and strangers

Challenge a friend or a random opponent. You have 72 hours for each move, with chat and reactions along the way.

The computer, or one phone

Play the computer, from easy to strong, or pass one phone across the table. Or race the clock alone.

Eight languages

English, Norwegian, Swedish, Dutch, Finnish, French, Italian and Spanish, each checked against an open word list. Or switch to Numbers or Sets.

Boards of every shape

From tight towers to open landscapes. Draw your own with up to 100 tiles, and share it with a code.

How it plays

1

Make a word

Tap the open tiles in order. Only tiles with nothing on top can be used.

2

Uncover the next layer

The tiles you used are gone, and the letters underneath come up. Keep up to two tiles in your pocket for later.

3

Score

Long words score more, bonus tiles double or triple, and clearing the whole board gives 50.
